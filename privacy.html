In one line

Nanopik collects no personal data. Nothing is sent to a server, there are no accounts, and no usage is recorded.

Effective 24 September 2026

What is not collected

  • Identity — name, email, phone number
  • Your photos, or what is inside them (location, capture time, and so on)
  • Usage, analytics, diagnostics, advertising identifiers
  • Device identifiers, location

Nanopik makes no network connections. There is no code in the app that sends anything to a server, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDK is included.

What stays on your Mac

The following is stored only in the app's own container on your Mac and is never transmitted.

  • Ratings — kept in the app's own database alongside file names.
  • Folder access — the security-scoped bookmarks macOS issues so the folders you chose can be reopened.
  • Settings — language, thumbnail size, slideshow interval and the like.
  • A log file — the app's own record, used to track down problems. It stays in the app's container and is truncated automatically.

Deleting the app removes all of the above along with its container. Your photos and photo folders are left untouched.

How your photo files are handled

Photos are read only from the folders you choose. The app writes to files in three cases, each of which you ask for:

  • when you save an adjustment
  • when you batch rename files
  • when you write corrected capture times into files

All three go through a confirmation and can be undone. Nothing else is ever added to your photo folder.

Children

Nanopik is not directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone.
